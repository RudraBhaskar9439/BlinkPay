# Social post draft

I built BlinkPay for @monad_xyz Spark: one signed USDC invoice, five ways to pay
from what you already own.

BlinkPay reads live wallet + DeFi positions, lets you say things like “preserve
MON,” and deterministically routes an exact payment through direct USDC, WMON,
ERC-4626 shares, or an atomic split.

The AI never signs or creates calldata. You approve every transaction, the
merchant receives the exact amount, and the receipt is onchain.

Live demo: https://blink-pay-web.vercel.app
Code: https://github.com/RudraBhaskar9439/BlinkPay
